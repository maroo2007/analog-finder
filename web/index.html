    The Moon is harder

    The lunar south pole is rougher than almost any dry land in Africa and the Americas. Its closest twins are in Chile, Argentina and Bolivia. We show the rank and say plainly how far off it still is.

    The targets are NASA's 9 Artemis III candidate regions. Artemis III is now a crewed test flight in Earth orbit; the first landing moved to Artemis IV (2028), and its site is not chosen yet.

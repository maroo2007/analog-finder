# Story page (web/index.html)

Mode: Persuade. Audience: Space Apps judges arriving cold (see PRODUCT.md).
Action: LAUNCH THE TWIN FINDER (explorer.html). Proof: Jezero to Egypt rank,
blind test, open data, honest limits. Content and storyboard pinned in
docs/WEBSITE_SPEC.md section 3. Quality bar chosen by the user:
endurosat.com/space-service, Apple product pages, NASA Eyes.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The category standard, played straight and at full craft: a cinematic
near-black scroll story where one WebGL planet changes identity scene by scene
under huge white caps. It keeps every convention and drops only the tells:
no eyebrow labels, no gradient text, no decorative glow halos.

OWN-WORLD: Cool near-black ground #06080b with a sparse, real-looking star
field; text #f3f5f7, secondary #8b95a1; hairlines white at 12%. One accent,
Earth blue #5aa9e6: chapter bar fill, primary button, progress bars. Mars
#e0703a and Moon #c4c9d1 appear only on their own globes, rims and data.
Archivo (self-hosted variable): display 800 weight, caps, tight; body 19 to
20 px. Square corners, square arrow-box icon, no shadows.

STORY: A judge understands the idea in scenes 1 to 4, believes the method and
the evidence in 5 to 12 (one ruler, four numbers, blind test, Egypt, rank
counter), reads the limits in 13 and 14, and clicks LAUNCH in 15.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Header strip at top: logo text left, Story, Twin Finder,
Egypt, Method right. FIND MARS ON EARTH in three lines of display caps, left
column, vertically centred, about 6rem; event line under it in secondary grey.
A Mars globe on the right, about 75vh across, lit from the upper left with an
orange rim, its lower edge low enough that scrolling turns it into the horizon.
The fixed LAUNCH THE TWIN FINDER button sits bottom right from the start.

FORM: the category standard (the standing exit; not on the ordered list);
seed: seed:ad24a211944f43cb. Signature interaction: the single fixed planet
that becomes Mars, a horizon, dotted Earth, Blue Marble Earth with Mars and the
Moon, and the Moon's south pole as you scroll, plus the rank odometer rolling
130,060 down to #1. Motion grammar: pinned scenes scrubbed to scroll with an
exponential ease, word-by-word text fill, nothing animates without the scroll.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
